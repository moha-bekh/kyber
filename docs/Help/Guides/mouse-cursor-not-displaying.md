# Mouse cursor not displayed
If you are using Kyber on a computer without a plugged-in mouse, Windows does not display the cursor by default.

Here is a workaround for this issue.

---

## Steps

**Video tutorial:**

<video controls width="100%" src="/uploads/6647ec4b806af2ae9a3c4eeddd182f22/mouse-key-video.mp4"></video>
---

### Step 1: Open Settings

Press these two keys **at the same time**:

**Windows** (the key with the ⊞ logo) **+ I**

A "Settings" window opens.

---

### Step 2: Go to "Accessibility"

Press **Tab** several times until "Home" is highlighted.

Then go down with the **keyboard arrows** to **Accessibility**.

Press **Enter**.

![Screenshot with Accessibility circled in red](/uploads/7c838f0a2f2eae4d39891051aa6c2b93/step-2.png)
---

### Step 3: Go to "Mouse"

Press **Tab** to move to the right-hand list.

Go down with the **keyboard arrows** to **Mouse**.

Press **Enter**.

![Screenshot with Mouse circled in red](/uploads/1026853855f37dedf0e096a6557d0ea3/step-3.png)

---

### Step 4: Enable "Mouse keys"

Press **Tab** until you reach the **"Mouse keys"** button.

Press **Space** to enable it.

The button turns **blue** = it's enabled ✓

![Screenshot with the enabled button (blue) circled](/uploads/e42c9905b7f42f77c3bdd49d648d4a6d/step-4.png)
---

## ✅ Done!

The mouse cursor is now visible on your screen.

---

⚠️ **Note:** If you unplug a mouse, you will need to repeat this procedure.
